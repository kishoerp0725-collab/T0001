Home / How It Works

Three steps. About ten minutes.

From "we have a workflow that eats our week" to a running AI employee — with you in control of every sensitive action.

01

Connect your world

Authorize ERPNext over OAuth + Frappe Assistant Core, then the apps your team already uses — Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Stripe, your bank portal. No code, no config files.

02

Hire the employee

Pick a template from the marketplace — or describe the job in plain English. You set the scope, the schedule and the approval thresholds (e.g. "hold anything over LKR 500k").

03

Watch, approve, relax

Follow every action on the live workspace board or in Slack. Sensitive actions pause for your one-click approval. Everything else ships while you get on with your day.

A day in the life

09:00 — the Invoice Chaser finds 4 overdue invoices and sends reminders. 09:12 — one is over your threshold: it waits on your Desk. 09:14 — you approve it from Slack. 17:40 — the Day is logged: 118 actions, 2 approvals, 0 manual steps.
